    In-Rack Solutions

    Power, cool, and connect the most demanding AI and compute systems with purpose-built in-rack integration. Supermicro delivers Coolant Distribution Units supporting up to 250kW of liquid cooling capacity, high-performance networking switches including NVIDIA InfiniBand and Ethernet solutions. Combined with 33kW Power Shelves and Battery Backup Units that provide 90 seconds of 48V DC power for application checkpointing, Supermicro's In-Rack Solutions deliver the thermal management, networking fabric, power delivery, and resilience required for next-generation AI data centers – all optimized for maximum rack density and operational efficiency.

    Learn More

    Coolant

    Supermicro Coolant is a high-performance, PG-based synthetic liquid coolant engineered specifically for direct-to-chip liquid cooling in high-density AI and HPC data centers, delivering long-term thermal and chemical stability, broad compatibility with metals, plastics, and elastomers (including EPDM and silicone), advanced multi-metal corrosion protection, and superior anti-bacterial performance.

    Read Datasheet

    CDUs and CDMs

    Coolant Distribution Units and Manifolds in various configurations, including vertical CDMs for higher server densities. In-Rack CDUs accommodate up to 250kW of cooling capacity for liquids up to 45°C, with redundant power supplies and pumps as standard for maximum reliability.

    Read Datasheet

    Rear Door Heat Exchanger

    Rear Door Heat Exchangers attach directly to server racks to remove heat at the source, significantly reducing data center air conditioning requirements while maintaining optimal operating temperatures for high-density AI infrastructure.

    View Product

    Cooling Hose Kit

    Flexible hose kits designed to connect liquid-cooled racks directly to data center primary water supply or cooling towers. Pre-installed hose kits enable plug-and-play, toolless deployment with seamless integration into new or existing facility piping.

    View Solution

    Power Shelves & BBUs

    33kW Power Shelves engineered for the latest generation of high-density AI racks, combined with Battery Backup Units providing 90 seconds of 48V DC power for application checkpointing during power failures – eliminating the need for complete application restarts.

    Read Datasheet

    Networking Switches

    NVIDIA Ethernet and InfiniBand platforms including NVIDIA Spectrum-X and Quantum-2 for optimized AI cluster networking, as well as qualified partner solutions. Supermicro Ethernet switches with up to 800 GbE link speeds and 51.2 Tbps throughput are also available for diverse workload requirements.

    View Product

    In-Row Solutions

    Scale your liquid cooling infrastructure with flexible in-row solutions designed for multi-rack deployments. Supermicro In-Row CDUs deliver up to 2.2MW of cooling capacity across multiple racks, while Liquid-to-Air Sidecars provide up to 500kW of standalone cooling with no external infrastructure modifications. For complete turnkey deployment, SuperCluster delivers pre-validated, plug-and-play multi-rack solutions with integrated networking fabric and rigorous L11/L12 testing—enabling seamless scaling from pilot projects to full production.

    Learn More

    Racks

    Supermicro-branded racks deliver a one-stop, end-to-end platform – from rack structure and system integration to cooling readiness and factory validation. With full in-house engineering and manufacturing, every rack is built to meet strict quality standards while ensuring seamless compatibility across the entire system.

    View Product

    In-Row CDU

    High-capacity In-Row Coolant Distribution Units designed to accommodate multiple high-powered liquid-cooled server racks, delivering up to 2.2MW of cooling capacity for scalable, multi-rack deployments in dense AI data center environments.

    Read Datasheet

    Liquid-to-air (L2A) Sidecar CDU

    Independent cooling units that convert hot liquid from servers back to cool liquid within the data center, providing up to 500kW of cooling capacity with no external infrastructure modifications – ideal for retrofitting and rapidly deploying liquid-cooled systems.

    Read Datasheet

    SuperCluster

    Highly customizable, plug-and-play cluster solutions with multiple racks and integrated networking fabric. Each system undergoes rigorous L11 or L12 validation testing before shipping, ensuring turnkey deployments that enable seamless scaling from pilot projects to full production AI infrastructure.

    View Solution

    Site Infrastructure Solutions

    Build a high-performance data center foundation with fully engineered cabling, power, and cooling infrastructure. Supermicro delivers optimized cabling design and implementation with complete documentation including routing, port mapping, and cable length optimization that reduces time-to-online, material costs, and labor. For site-level thermal management, Supermicro offers water cooling towers in scalable configurations and dry cooling towers that eliminate water consumption – all designed for easy installation and parallel expansion as your infrastructure grows.

    Learn More

    Water Cooling Towers

    Primary liquid loop facility supporting scalable cooling capacity from 1 MW to 50 MW or more with modular designs for faster time-to-operation. Closed-loop systems with EC fan design and N+1 submersible pumps ensure energy-efficient, reliable operation with reduced power and water usage versus conventional air-cooled systems.

    Read Datasheet

    Dry Cooler

    Delivers low PUE/WUE cooling for water-constrained regions, with scalable capacities from 1 MW to over 50 MW. Adiabatic-assisted mode enables air pre-cooling for superior performance in high-temperature environments. Closed-loop design keeps coolant clean and non-corrosive, ensuring exceptional efficiency, minimal maintenance, and long-term reliability.

    Read Datasheet

    Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)

    Delivers a self-contained, liquid-cooled 1.5 MW/3.1 MWh energy storage solution purpose-built for mission-critical AI data centers. Safe LFP cells with advanced cell-level management, multi-layer fire barriers, clean-agent suppression, and explosion prevention ensure maximum safety and reliability, while peak shaving and power smoothing reduce energy costs and stabilize fluctuating AI workloads.

    Read Datasheet

    Cabling

    Optimized traffic engineering with complete cabling design, documentation, and implementation services including routing, port mapping, and cable length optimization. Engineered solutions reduce time-to-online, material costs, and labor while ensuring high-performance data flow for multi-rack deployments.

    Read Brochure

    Management Software Suite

    From bare metal to AI workloads, SuperCloud Software Suite as part of Data Center Building Block Solutions delivers end-to-end management for modern data centers. Unify infrastructure control, automate deployment pipelines, empower developers with self-service AI tools, and optimize multi-tenant GPU cloud operations—all purpose-built for the scale and complexity of AI factories and hyperscale environments.

    SuperCloud Composer® (SCC)

    SuperCloud Composer delivers unified rack-scale and liquid cooling management across servers, networks, PDUs, CDUs, and 3rd-party systems. It provides power management, advanced leak detection, protection, and alerts to safeguard GPU and cooling infrastructure. With observability and tuning at scale, SuperCloud Composer manages 20K+ hosts through a single portal.

    Learn More

    SuperCloud Automation Center (SCAC)

    SuperCloud Automation Center delivers pre-built, enterprise-grade automation for data center and edge infrastructure. It covers everything from firmware and OS provisioning to Kubernetes and AI workload enablement while ensuring security, scalability and governance.

    Learn More

    SuperCloud Developer Console (SDX)

    SuperCloud Developer Console is a developer-oriented AI experience console that brings GPU as a Service (GPUaaS), Kubernetes, Machine Learning pipelines, and data services into one workspace. Unlike other infrastructure-only consoles, it delivers turnkey developer agility with self-service provisioning, AI-native workflows, and built in observability, empowering teams to go from code to model faster and safer.

    Learn More

    SuperCloud Director (SCD)

    SuperCloud Director delivers multi-tenant AI cloud control with integrated bare metal, Ethernet & Infiniband network multi-tenancy, and storage management, optimizing performance, security, and developer agility. Unlike generic cloud management tools, this software is purpose-built for GPU as a Service (GPUaaS), AI factory deployments, and hyperscale operations.
